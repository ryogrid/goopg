## 2. What changed

goopg never ran PG's `canonicalize_qual` (`process_duplicate_ors`, prepqual.c).
Q19's entire WHERE is an OR of three conjunctions that each repeat the join
clause `p_partkey = l_partkey`; upstream hoists it — and the three other
conjuncts common to all arms — out of the OR before distributing the qual.
Without that, goopg charged the join clause once as the equi-join key and once
more per arm at DEFAULT_EQ_SEL, and left `l_shipmode IN (…)`,
`l_shipinstruct = '…'` and `p_size >= 1` trapped inside the OR where nothing
priced them at all. Full statement: `docs/design/leftdeep-joins/09` §5.9.

The plan now carries PG's own Q19 shape:

```
Hash Join (INNER)  rows=309  (actual rows=131.00)
      Hash Cond: (lineitem.l_partkey = part.p_partkey)
      Filter: ((part.p_size >= 1) AND (lineitem.l_shipmode = ANY ('AIR', 'AIR REG'))
               AND (lineitem.l_shipinstruct = 'DELIVER IN PERSON'))
  ->  Seq Scan on public.lineitem
        Filter: ((l_shipmode = ANY ('AIR', 'AIR REG')) AND (l_shipinstruct = 'DELIVER IN PERSON'))
  ->  Seq Scan on public.part
        Filter: (p_size >= 1)
```

against `2026-08-05-p56g.plans.txt`, where both scans were bare and the join
printed no filter at all.

## 3. Why only two queries, stated so it is not read as a full sweep

`canonicalizeQual` returns its argument unchanged unless the qual contains an
OR, so it is a structural no-op on every OR-free query. Three of the 22 TPC-H
texts contain `or`; Q15's is `CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW`. Q12 and Q19 are
therefore the complete set of TPC-H queries this change can reach, and both were
measured. **The other 19 were not re-run** and no claim is made that their
numbers moved or held — the claim is that the pass cannot reach them.

Q12 is the useful control rather than a formality: its OR is
`(o_orderpriority = '1-URGENT' or o_orderpriority = '2-HIGH')`, two bare
equalities with no conjunct in common, which is `process_duplicate_ors`'s
no-winners path. It came back bit-identical (est 45 793 → 46 222 is ANALYZE
sampling noise between sessions, well inside §1's ~5 % noise floor).
