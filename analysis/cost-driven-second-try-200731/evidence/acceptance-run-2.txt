## Q5 regression mechanism (-0013 self-inflicted)

Cost-driven Q5 EXPLAIN at this HEAD (captured post-run, byte-stable across two
probes): top HashJoin probes the FULL 6M-row (lineitem ⋈ orders) intermediate
against the 37.5K dim tree — i.e. all 6M rows cross TWO binary hash-join
operators. At the profiled ~117µs/row binary-HJ cost (see memory/evidence for
Q9, 02-premise-audit) that is ~700s — consistent with the >600s hang. Run-1's
8.15s winner joined the selective dim tree BEFORE expanding to the full
lineitem stream. Attempt 1's >2M-row DP build penalty + attempt 2's
inner_pages I/O charge re-ranked exactly that winning order out: the 6M-row
intermediate is now preferred as PROBE side (penalties charge builds, not
probes), so the DP dodges large builds by routing the large stream through
extra probe passes — the pathology the penalty was meant to prevent, relocated.
