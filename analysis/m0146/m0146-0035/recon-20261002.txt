    goopg's redo starts there although startup never reads backup_label (PG renames it to
    backup_label.old after recovery; goopg leaves it in place).

    new content, and the old content of xid 5's lane is already committed.
  - None of the startup-reload / CLOG files changed between the 2026-09-29 incident and today.
