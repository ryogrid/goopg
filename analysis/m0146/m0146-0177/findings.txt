M0146-0177 — the ordered-paths step offers a full Sort beside the Incremental Sort (TPC-DS Q60), 2026-10-13

Cause: addOrderedPaths' presorted arm (PG create_ordered_paths: "We'll just do a sort if there are no presorted
keys and an incremental sort when there are presorted keys") required input.node. The grouping election
(upperorderedgrouping.go) and the DISTINCT election offer node-less PathAgg / PathDistinct candidates, so a
presorted candidate fell to the full-Sort arm; addIncrementalSortPaths also skips node-less inputs, so no
Incremental Sort was offered at all. Trace (GOOPG_PGSHAPED_DP_TRACE=1, Q60 SF0.25): seed kind=PathAgg ncommon=1,
classes-presorted=1, node=false -> upper.ordered.sort only.

Fix: addOrderedPaths takes statsNode (the node the sort runs over, for cost_incremental_sort's
estimate_num_groups); createOrderedPaths passes its input, the grouping election the aggregate's ordered input
node, the DISTINCT election its distinct node (and its winner switch now accepts an Incremental Sort).

Sibling found by the fire set: upperNarrowChildren (B-01c upper narrowing descent) listed Sort but not
IncrementalSort, so Q54 — whose top became an Incremental Sort — kept its aggregate un-narrowed and its inner Sort
printed `sum(store_sales.ss_ext_sales_price) / 50` instead of PG's `my_revenue.revenue / 50`. Arm added.
