So PG has no ordered unique-outer nested loop to evict the hash semi join.

Why: bisected on the debug build (variants*.sql). PG keeps the semijoin (does not reduce it to an inner join) under
ROLLUP / CUBE / `s_state, ROLLUP(s_county)` / two grouping sets, even when s_state is not grouped; it reduces it under
a plain GROUP BY and under GROUPING SETS ((a,b)) (planned as plain GROUP BY). Mechanism: pull_up_simple_subquery sets
REPLACE_WRAP_ALL when parse->groupingSets (prepjointree.c) — every pulled output becomes a PlaceHolderVar, so the
semijoin's RHS operand is no plain Var: rel_is_distinct_for cannot prove uniqueness (no reduce_unique_semijoins) and
create_unique_path cannot take its free UNIQUE_PATH_NOOP arm (a paid sort/hash UniquePath has pathkeys NIL).

goopg had two defects:
  1. SemiRhsDistinct (the NOOP unique-ification) and pulledSemiRhsIsUnique ignored grouping sets.
  2. nestLoopPlainOuters returned the RAW cheapest outer for JOIN_UNIQUE_OUTER (plain and materialised NL), not the
     unique-ified path PG's match_unsorted_outer uses for every nested loop of that branch — harmless only while the
     unique path was the free NOOP; with a paid one it joins RHS duplicates and keeps the raw ordering.
